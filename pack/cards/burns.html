🔥 Burns

1. Call 112 for large / face-hand-genital / chemical / electrical burns.
2. Cool under cool running water ≥ 20 minutes (not ice).
3. Remove jewellery/tight clothes if not stuck to skin.
4. Cover loosely with clean non-fluffy cloth or cling film.
5. No butter, oil, toothpaste, or bursting blisters.
6. Watch for shock — call 112.
