🦵 Sprain / twist

1. Rest the joint — do not walk on a bad ankle if painful.
2. Ice in cloth 10–15 min (not direct on skin).
3. Light compression if you know how — watch for numbness/blue tips.
4. Elevate when sitting/lying.
5. Extreme pain, deformity, numbness, or cannot bear weight → urgent care / 112.
6. Follow up with a clinic — fractures can look like sprains.
