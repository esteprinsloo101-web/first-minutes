❤️ Chest pain — CALL 112 NOW

1. Call 112 NOW. Do not drive yourself if severe.
2. Sit upright, rest, loosen tight clothing.
3. Help with prescribed heart medicine if conscious + prescribed.
4. Aspirin only if conscious, able to swallow, not allergic, and previously advised — else wait for EMS.
5. Note time symptoms started. Stay calm.
6. If unresponsive / not breathing normally: CPR if trained.

General first-aid wait tips only. Not a doctor, nurse, EMS, or medical advice. Does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or triage. Call 112 in an emergency — do not wait on this card. Adults 18+. © First Minutes
